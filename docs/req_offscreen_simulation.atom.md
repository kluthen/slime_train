# Off-screen simulation

## INTENT
Define how slimes and objects behave once off screen, since physics only runs near the screen.

## THE RULE / LOGIC
Physics runs only for slimes on or near the screen. Off screen, a train slime is a position along the loop moving at a deterministic pace; when the view comes near it, it is spawned just outside the view and physics takes over. A free slime that leaves the screen is placed on the nearest point of its area's route back and follows it at the same deterministic pace. If no route back is near, it is lost: it heads straight for the loop when the loop is near; otherwise it stays where it is until the lost timer (10 s, then 1 min) makes it due a move to the start of the loop, which it gets at its turn, one move at a time, landing on a free spot there. Fusion and waking happen only on screen. Baskets keep counting weight off screen and can fill there. On screen too, a resting pile of slimes stops simulating, contacts included, until something disturbs it, and a disturbance wakes only the resting slimes it touches, never the rest of their pile; this is the first fallback for performance, since most of a big crowd on screen is expected to be a still pile. A pile rests once every slime in it has stayed within about a pixel of where it started counting; a big pile of base slimes in the open may take about a minute to rest. Sleepers don't simulate until something touches them, slimes in a full basket use a simplified state, and slimes on screen may use fewer points when zoomed out, and, in normal play, when many slimes are active and the device can't keep up (rule_crowd_detail_only_under_load).

## TECHNICAL INTERFACE
Parented to req_loop_and_world. Interacts with rule_left_alone_and_lost (the lost timer) and req_switch_basket_gate_set (off-screen basket filling).

Settled by the user (2026-10-07, approved as proposed; tuning values are the values to build to, still to try): crowd detail, slimes given fewer ring points when many slimes are active, not only when zoomed out. The crowd count is the ACTIVE slimes that are not sleepers on a tick; resting and parked slimes don't count, and the count comes from the simulation's state only, never from the frame rate or a measured time. Detail levels 0 to 3 give 12, 10, 8, 6 points to a size-1 slime (15, 12, 10, 8 at size 2; 18, 15, 12, 9 at size 3). The level rises at 20, 30 and 40 active slimes and falls only at 15, 25 and 35, so rings never reshape back and forth. The level used is the higher of the zoom's and the lower of the crowd's and a detail ceiling (0 to 3); zoomed out gives at least level 2. The ceiling is an input handed to the simulation at a tick boundary, like the tilt; it is neither state nor saved. With the simulation's own default ceiling, 3 (the crowd's level as is), or a ceiling fixed at 0, the detail comes from the simulation's state alone and runs repeat exactly (req_test_level_and_test_mode). In normal play, and always in a release build, the ceiling follows the device's measured load instead (rule_crowd_detail_only_under_load): a device that keeps up keeps full ring points whatever the crowd, and normal play's detail then depends on the measured load, the ceiling and its hold after a bounce, so it doesn't repeat from run to run. Only ACTIVE rings are reshaped, so a reshape never wakes a resting pile, and a slime waking or unparking takes the current level on that tick. Pile slimes (in a basket, or asleep at bedtime) stop at level 2. Implemented in src/sim/offscreen.gd and src/sim/slime_bodies.gd (the ceiling: Offscreen.detail_ceiling, handed over by src/main.gd from src/platform/load_meter.gd); saved as described in req_persistence_and_saves.

Settled by the user (the direction, 2026-10-03; the details approved as proposed, 2026-10-07): the local wake. A disturbance wakes only the resting slimes it touches, never the rest of their pile, and never a sleeper (sleepers still wake only on contact with an awake slime). Every way a resting slime wakes goes through this one single-slime wake, each trigger made local: a basket letting a slime go, a touch faster than the wake speed (30 px/s: a hop, a landing, a neighbour moving), a slime moved to the start of the loop, a fusion, a split, and a slime taken out of the level wake the resting slimes touching the slime concerned; a call wakes the resting slimes within its radius; a trapdoor, gate or lid opening or shutting wakes the resting slimes within 80 px of it; a state change (bedtime, sunrise, a basket catching or releasing a slime) wakes the slime whose state changed; a tilt change wakes every resting slime, each by itself. The rest of a pile wakes only if a woken slime then touches it faster than the wake speed. A woken slime rests again with whatever group it then forms. Which slimes may rest is unchanged (pile slimes only: in a basket, or asleep at bedtime), and nothing is added to the save. If piles turned out to churn, the fallback is to wake the touched slimes' touching neighbours too, one step, never the whole pile; it was not needed, is not built, and is kept as the fallback. Measured on the build, on the test level: draining basket 3, piles woken whole went from 6 to 0 (at most 9 pile slimes woken in one tick), and with basket 3 holding 59 of its 60 slimes on screen the phone-emulated frame rate went from 25.4 to 35.0 fps. Implemented in src/sim/slime_bodies.gd (_wake_at and the wake calls built on it), src/sim/offscreen.gd (call and tilt) and src/sim/frontier_sets.gd (doors and releases).

Settled by the user (the direction, 2026-10-02; the wording and details approved as proposed, 2026-10-07): the move to the start of the loop that the lost timer triggers is not immediate. A lost slime, like a stuck or stalled one, waits its turn in one loop-start queue, one move at a time, the next turn 30 to 120 ticks (0.5 to 2 s) after the last, and lands on a random free spot on the loop's first 240 px; meanwhile it carries on as it would, and leaves the queue without a move if it comes back on screen. A train slime's 60 s stall count pauses while it is parked off screen (rule_stalled_train_slime_moved_to_start; which parked train slimes the pause covers is still open, the build pausing it for every one). Implemented in src/sim/offscreen.gd (the off-screen count), src/sim/loop_start_queue.gd and src/sim/train.gd; nothing is added to the save.

## EXPECTATION
A basket filled while off screen plays its reward and opens the gate when it comes into view (definition of done item 10). A pile stops simulating only once each of its slimes has stayed within about a pixel of where it started counting, and when something disturbs it the resting slimes it touches simulate again, not the rest of their pile.
