# Early player persona (P3)

## INTENT
Describe the secondary persona, a roughly 4-year-old who plays more deliberately, and how v1 serves her intentional-play goals.

## THE RULE / LOGIC
P3 represents friends' children, about 4 years old, on a similar phone to P1's, who play more on purpose than P1 does. Her goals and how v1 serves them: (1) Play on purpose - call, fuse, fill a basket, open the next section - served fully by sleepers, fusion, the switch-plus-basket gate set, and the level's four sections. (2) Explore off the loop to find more slimes, served fully by exploration branches whose hints are visible from the loop, and by the call dragging the camera so she can look off the loop without a joystick.

## TECHNICAL INTERFACE
Restated from the master spec's persona table (section 2).

## EXPECTATION
A child matching this persona can complete the sequence of calling, fusing, filling a basket and opening a section using only the documented controls, with tilt never required.
