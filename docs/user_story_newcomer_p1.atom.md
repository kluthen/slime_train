# Newcomer persona (P1)

## INTENT
Describe the primary persona, a 3-year-old newcomer to video games who can't read, and how v1 serves her goals.

## THE RULE / LOGIC
P1 is a 3-year-old girl who has never played a video game and can't read. She plays on a Samsung Galaxy S20 FE, at home, in the daytime, as a reward or after school. This is v1's primary persona: its goals are served fully wherever possible. Her goals and how v1 serves them: (1) See something pleasant happen and keep watching it, served fully by the train running with no input, the idle camera, and screensaver mode. (2) Touch the screen and see the world answer straight away, served fully by tap-to-call anywhere, a ripple on every tap, and slimes in range turning toward the touch. (3) Find out on her own what touching does, with no one to explain and no reading, served by the ripple feedback and a wordless pulsing hint that appears near the first sleeper on the very first play if she hasn't called yet; the first sleeper sits close to the first awake slime to make this discoverable. This goal is served fully in design but still to be confirmed in playtests. (4) Never fail, never get stuck, served fully by having no failure states, a loop that runs with no input, and a lost slime that comes back on its own.

A playtest with a child matching this persona finds the call on her own, with no adult explaining it (goal 3, definition of done item 32).
