## Observations

1. All three DoD 30 targets pass on the real phone in the cold window. The phone runs about 1.4 to 1.8 times the phone emulation's fps, so the emulation is pessimistic. Against the 2026-09-30 session, basket 3 went from 4–5 fps to 56–59 fps.
2. Heat played no part: thermal status 0 throughout, and the battery stayed at or below 34 °C. The fps follows the load (the awake count and the largest cluster), not the time. stress-dense and stress-moving climb to 59 fps once their crowds settle (after 60 s and 180 s).
3. The weak spot is s3-basket-59of60 after about 151 s. The PERF lines switch to section 1, where 90 to 115 slimes are awake in one cluster of 80 to 100, and the fps drops to 18–23 for the rest of the run (2 ticks per frame at 22.5 ms per tick). That is below 30 and is not the case the DoD target names. The likely cause is the train coming back round, but that is not checked.
4. A tick costs about 11 ms even with only 20 to 30 physics slimes (warm windows of stress-dense and stress-moving). There is a floor that doesn't depend on the physics count (the train, the loop and the hops are the likely candidates), and two ticks per frame can't fit in 16.7 ms.
5. Short hops make up 77 to 96 % of the train hops while the crowds are dense, and fall to 56 to 78 % once settled.
6. Incidents: the first stress-dense try launched behind the phone's lock screen (the PIN lock had come back when run 1's force-stop applied a pending auto-lock) and logged nothing. The first stress-moving try lost adb while the phone was being unlocked. Both are discarded and kept as `FAILED-*` folders. Both runs were redone after the unlock.
7. The 19w on-device save-wipe check was **not done**. The permission system refused the `--free-play --wipe-save` run, and then even a read-only listing of the saves folder. Nothing ran and the saves were not touched. The last listing (before the attempt) showed test.json, test.json.bak and test.json.v1 dated 2026-09-30, parent.json kept. All three steps (the wipe run, the refusal with a fixture, the resume) are left to the user.
