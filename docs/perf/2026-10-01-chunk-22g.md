All of them are train slimes of size 1, all PARKED (off-screen, settled), supported, not holding, from all five species. 64 of them fire at once in the 3600-4200 window (the 60 s net counted from the fixture's start). They sit in the bowl: loop distance 15,500 to 17,500, x 15,500 to 17,250, below basket 3's box (x from 18,109) and not inside it (the gap to every basket is over 400 px). That is the queue parked in front of basket 3, never woken.
- The other 22 stalls are at 7200-7800, at loop distance 0 to 500 (the loop start). These slimes were moved to the start by the first wave, parked there, and stalled again 60 s later.
- The stuck moves are 305 moves on only 61 slimes (about 5 each), all between ticks 8400 and 10000. All are train slimes of size 1, active, supported. Every one is at the loop start (distance 0 to 500 or about 38,000, x 0 to 500), paired with another size-1 train slime of a different species, often the same few partners (ids 14, 11, 4, 10). 54 % are holding.
- Several moved slimes land on the exact same point (x 210, y 409, distance 34): LoopStart.move drops a stuck slime onto the start pile, it gets stuck again 2 s later and is moved again. This is a re-stuck cycle at the loop start, not a jam in the bowl or the basket.
- Nothing happens near basket 3 itself: one stuck move is near x 17,750 and none is inside a box. s3's stall and stuck counts come from the parked bowl queue (stall net) and from the start pile (stuck cycle), both present since 22e.

## Observations

- ON 300 px changes stress-moving only. On s3-basket it changes nothing up to 2,400 ticks and nothing at all for seed 1 (seed 2 diverges late: stuck 172 -> 189).
- On stress-moving no length is clearly better. The stall totals (s1 + s2) are OFF 40, ON 300 39, ON 150 54, ON 100 32, and the spread between seeds is larger than the gaps between configurations. ON 100 has the fewest stalls; ON 300 has the most hops on s1.
- ON makes the train busier, not calmer. It adds 20 to 64 % more hops in 2,400 ticks, the same or a higher short-hop share (92 to 95 %), Physics +13 to +21, fewer resting slimes and a larger cluster mean. Front-hop share drops (18/19 % -> 11 to 17 %).
- Cost: per-tick +1.5 % (300 px) / +4 % (100 px) on desktop. That is mostly the extra awake slimes (physics 103 -> 119/121), not the bucket bookkeeping.
- The bowl never drains on stress-moving in 10,000 ticks under any configuration. On s3 the bowl drains by the stall net at tick 3600, and the stuck count is a re-stuck cycle at the loop start (section 5).
