## Diagnostic

The probe ([`probe/hold_probe.gd`](2026-10-01-chunk-22f/probe/hold_probe.gd))
replicates `Simulation.step()` and `Train.steer()` call for call, so each
decision is read in the exact state its own steer sees (22e's probe read
them before steer, and 24 starts came out wrong when a holder ahead began
in the same tick). A decision is a due train slime standing on something
(no hold yet), or a holder at a re-check or at the cap; a due slime aimed
one tick before its hop fires ("pending") is not counted twice. Queue
position: train slimes ahead along the loop within 300 px (22e's bins).
"Ahead along the loop": a train slime by its progress, any other slime by
projecting its centre onto the loop within 400 px of the slime's progress
(a proxy; in these runs every counted slime was a train slime).

## Part 2

**A. The dip pin.** The cause was ours: `Fusion._gathering` let a slime
on a dip's floor wait "as long as it likes" for a partner directly behind
it, and a holder never comes, so the slime stayed pinned (its hop timer
held at `DIP_HOLD_SECONDS`, 0.25 s) for ever. Now a holding partner
directly behind is waited for at most `DIP_WAIT_TICKS` (5 s, counted from
when the slime's progress last advanced), like a partner with other slimes
between (proposed). And the hold guard counts a dip-pinned train slime as
waiting (`TrainHold.dip_pinned`, read from the hop timer, saved state).
Over 10,000 ticks at 0.5: 97 / 94 stall moves, 609 / 702 stuck moves,
1693 / 1660 hops: the pin fixed, the bowl still stuck behind its front's
crowd.

**B. The occupancy sweep.** `HOLD_OCCUPANCY` from 0.5 to 0.7, on the tree
after step A (before step 5), `thru.gd`, seeds 1 / 2. The rule set before
the sweep: the lowest value with zero stall moves, zero stuck moves and
zero guard releases over 10,000 ticks; if none, the fewest stall moves,
