## Observations

1. Cap OFF, stress-moving: D152 cuts the stall moves from 19/21 to 6/4, with stuck still at 0 and hops slightly up (3362/3289 against 3192/3170). The bowl drains a little slower, with bowl_n at the end 67/70 against 53, because the stall net no longer moves the held slimes on.
2. Cap ON, stress-moving: the train jams. Hops drop to 819/787 (a quarter of OFF), and only about 44 slimes leave the bowl in 10,000 ticks (bowl_n 96/101, against 0 before D152). Before D152 the bowl emptied through the stall net (94 stalls, 715 stuck). Holding slimes now never stall, so nothing breaks the jam. In the phone-emulation ON run, about 96 slimes are holding at any time on average (1-124). In the 10,000-tick runs, 7 buckets stay over cap for the whole run (load 31-35 against a cap of 12).
3. s3-basket: D152 changes nothing material. Seed 1 OFF matches the reference exactly (87/305), and seed 2 is close (85/199 against 86/172). The stalls are still the pile of parked slimes before basket 3, moved by the stall net at 3600-4200. The stuck count still comes from the re-stuck loop at the loop start from about tick 7800. The cap makes no clear difference to stall or stuck (only 3-4 bucket holds).
4. Phone emulation, cap OFF: within the references (s3-basket 25.4 fps, 14.5 ms per tick; stress-moving 13.5 fps). Cap ON: s3-basket is about 7 % slower (23.6 fps, 16.6 ms per tick). stress-moving is slightly faster (14.2 fps, Physics 101 against 124), because so many slimes hold.
5. The phone runs are 62 s, which is about 2000-3600 ticks. They don't reach the s3-basket pile at about 3600 ticks or the late jams. Only the 10,000-tick runs show those.
6. Nothing crashed or misbehaved: all 12 runs exited cleanly, and no Godot process is left running.
